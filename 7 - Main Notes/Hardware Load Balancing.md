# Hardware Load Balancing

Physical devices designed specifically for load balancing tasks

Specialized hardware components, such as 
- Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs) 
- Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs)

High performance and throughput, also large volumes of traffic and multiple protocols. Also are Expensive and have limited Scalability
